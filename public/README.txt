ANISHKA CHAPTER 21 — YOUR PRIVATE CONTENT

MUSIC
1. Put your chosen music file in this folder.
2. Name it birthday.mp3 (or change src/personalize.js to match the filename).

PHOTOS
1. Create a folder named photos inside public.
2. Put your photos inside public/photos/.
3. Open src/personalize.js and uncomment/edit the birthdayPhotos list with your photo filenames.
Example:
export const birthdayPhotos = [
  '/photos/anishka-1.jpg',
  '/photos/anishka-2.jpg',
  '/photos/anishka-3.jpg',
];

Visitors cannot upload or replace photos/music from the website. They can scroll, tap/click the page to create floating emojis, and use the one opening button. Music autoplay depends on browser policy; the opening tap retries playback.
